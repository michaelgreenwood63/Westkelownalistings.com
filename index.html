Why West Kelowna

The Okanagan's most liveable shoreline, without the Kelowna price tag.

West Kelowna stretches from the vineyard benches of Lakeview Heights down to the lakeshore along Westside Road, with Glenrosa, Shannon Lake, Smith Creek, Westbank Centre, and West Kelowna Estates filling out the family-friendly interior. Wineries, beaches, and mountain-bike trails sit minutes from home, with Downtown Kelowna a short bridge crossing away.

Mark Jontz & Associates has tracked this market for over two decades — the kind of context that matters when you're choosing a street, not just a city.

West Kelowna's seven distinct pockets

From vineyard-view benches to lakefront walking paths — every pocket of West Kelowna has its own character.

↗
Lakeview Heights
Vineyard-draped benches with sweeping lake views, home to several of the region's wineries.
↗
Glenrosa
One of West Kelowna's established residential communities, with roots dating to the 19th century.
↗
Shannon Lake
Home to the only 18-hole golf course in the City of West Kelowna, built around the lake of the same name.
↗
Smith Creek
A quieter residential pocket in West Kelowna's southern cluster, near Glenrosa and Shannon Lake.
↗
Westbank Centre
The historic heart of the city — shops, cafes, the Westbank Museum, and the civic core.
↗
West Kelowna Estates
An elevated residential area in the city's northern cluster, inland from the lakeshore.
↗
Westside Road
The lakeshore route north toward Bear Creek Provincial Park, with some of the area's quietest waterfront.

Yes — West Kelowna has been its own incorporated city since 2007, across the lake from Kelowna via the William R. Bennett Bridge.
