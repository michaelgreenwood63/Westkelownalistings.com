Westside Road

A lakeshore stretch running north from West Kelowna along Okanagan Lake, home to Bear Creek Provincial Park.

About & History

Lakeshore living, with a provincial park next door.

Officially named "Westside Road/Bear Creek Road" by the City of West Kelowna, this neighbourhood follows the road of the same name along the west side of Okanagan Lake, continuing northwest toward Fintry and the North Okanagan. It's one of the more scenic, lower-density stretches within the city, defined as much by the lake and the park beside it as by any town centre.

Bear Creek Provincial Park sits directly along this stretch — established in 1981 and expanded to its current 178 hectares (440 acres) in 1988, it's long been one of the more heavily used regional parks in the Okanagan (BC Parks recorded roughly 240,000 visits in a 2017–18 reporting period; current figures should be confirmed with BC Parks directly).

Location & Geography

The northern cluster's lakeshore edge.

The City of West Kelowna groups Westside Road with West Kelowna Estates, Lakeview Heights, and several smaller areas as its northern neighbourhood cluster. Unlike those more built-up pockets, Westside Road is defined by its length along the lakeshore, running north out of the main built-up area of the city toward Fintry.

It's a longer drive back to Westbank Centre and Highway 97C than the more central neighbourhoods, which is part of the trade-off for direct lakeshore and park-adjacent living.
