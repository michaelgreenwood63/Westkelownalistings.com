Grandview Terrace

A leasehold, family-friendly rancher-style townhome community in Westbank Centre, within walking distance of everyday shopping.

About This Building

Rancher-style townhomes, walking distance to everything.

Grandview Terrace, at 2210 Louie Drive, is a rancher-style townhome development in Westbank Centre. Units we've tracked range from 2-bedroom to 3-bedroom homes, several with vaulted ceilings, gas fireplaces, and private backyard patios. The community is within walking distance of Walmart, Real Canadian Superstore, Canadian Tire, and other everyday shopping, and offers RV/snowbird storage.

Grandview Terrace is a leasehold property per the listings we've tracked. We have not found explicit confirmation of whether the underlying land is Westbank First Nation land — don't assume either way (note this is a separate, distinct complex from Westlake Gardens at 2250 Louie Drive on the same street). Confirm specific lease terms directly, and see our WFN leasehold guide for general leasehold-buying considerations in this area.
