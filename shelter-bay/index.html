Shelter Bay

An exclusive new waterfront community at 2100 Campbell Place, with lake, bridge, and city views from multiple listings.

About This Building

New waterfront townhomes near the bridge.

Shelter Bay is a newer townhome community at 2100 Campbell Place. Real listings describe corner end units with exposed architectural beams, designer kitchens, and lake/bridge/city views, plus private elevators and rooftop terraces (one confirmed at 562 sq ft) on larger units. Short-term rentals are permitted on at least some units per MLS remarks.

MLS data lists this building as leasehold. Lakeview Heights, where Shelter Bay sits, is not part of the Westbank First Nation reserve lands that most of this site's other leasehold buildings (concentrated around Westbank Centre) sit on — so this is very likely a different, non-WFN leasehold arrangement. The exact landlord/lease structure for Shelter Bay is not yet confirmed; buyers should get full lease terms directly before relying on anything beyond what's stated here.
