Paramount Peak

Brand-new freehold townhomes at 2509 Paramount Peak Drive, part of the Tallus Ridge development in Shannon Lake.

About This Building

New-build townhomes in Tallus Ridge.

Paramount Peak is a new freehold townhome development at 2509 Paramount Peak Drive, part of Tallus Ridge, one of Shannon Lake's fastest-growing modern subdivisions. Real listings describe the “Redtail” floor plan specifically: two storeys over a walkout basement, 4 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, roughly 2,290 sq ft of living space, and a double side-by-side garage.

The main floor layout includes a powder room, open kitchen with island, dining area, and living room with an electric fireplace opening onto a covered deck with valley and mountain views. Pre-construction-style lot numbering seen on listings (e.g. “Prop SL 3”) suggests this was sold in phases as new construction; exact completion date isn't independently confirmed.
