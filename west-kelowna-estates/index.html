West Kelowna Estates

An elevated, inland residential neighbourhood in West Kelowna's northern cluster.

History & Character

A quieter corner of the city's north side.

West Kelowna Estates sits in the newer, more residential half of the city's geography, inland and elevated above the lakeshore.

Where the southern cluster (Westbank Centre, Glenrosa, Shannon Lake) carries most of the area's older, pre-incorporation history, West Kelowna Estates is part of the city's more recently developed northern residential expansion.

Location & Geography

Part of the northern cluster.

West Kelowna Estates belongs to the city's northern neighbourhood grouping, alongside Lakeview Heights and Westside Road. It sits inland from the lakeshore, in the more elevated residential stretch of that cluster.

Highway 97C connects the whole northern cluster back toward Westbank Centre and, via the William R. Bennett Bridge, across to downtown Kelowna.
