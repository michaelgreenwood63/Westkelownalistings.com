West Kelowna · Northern Neighbourhoods

West Kelowna Estates

A hillside neighbourhood perched directly north of Rose Valley, famed for lake proximity, elevated views, and the fastest commute to downtown Kelowna in the city.

History & Character

Built into the bedrock, facing the lake.

West Kelowna Estates is an established, quiet residential enclave built directly into the bedrock and pine slopes along Westlake Road. Housing ranges from classic 1970s-90s multi-level family homes on large lots to custom executive hillside builds with cliffside decks, modern townhomes, and low-rise condos along the lower access points.

Positioned directly across the water from downtown Kelowna, many properties here have sweeping views of Okanagan Lake, Knox Mountain, and the Kelowna skyline, which is exactly why it's widely considered the most commute-friendly neighbourhood in West Kelowna. Residents can drop down to Highway 97 and cross the William R. Bennett Bridge into downtown Kelowna in under 8-10 minutes.

Location & Geography

Directly north of Rose Valley.

West Kelowna Estates sits on the west side of Highway 97, perched directly north of Rose Valley along Westlake Road, distinct from Lakeview Heights, which is the only one of the seven neighbourhoods on the east side. Shannon Lake sits further south, with Westside Road (the northernmost neighbourhood) continuing north from here.

Highway 97C connects back toward Westbank Centre and, via the William R. Bennett Bridge, across to downtown Kelowna.

Amenities

What's close by

No school sits directly inside the neighbourhood's residential core, but Rose Valley and Westside schools in Central Okanagan School District #23 are all close by.

Schools (Central Okanagan Public Schools, District #23)

Elementary

Rose Valley Elementary

K–5, about 3 minutes away in the neighbouring Rose Valley subdivision.

Elementary

Mar Jok Elementary

K–5, nearby in Rose Valley, with a dual-track building and modern sports fields.

Middle School

Constable Neil Bruce Middle

Grades 6–8, down Highway 97 on Daimler Drive, with regular school bus service.

Secondary

Mount Boucherie Secondary

Grades 9–12, the primary public high school serving West Kelowna.

Parks & Recreation

Regional Park

Rose Valley Regional Park

Wraps around the west side of the neighbourhood, a 250-hectare park with dozens of km of trails for hiking, trail running, dog walking, and singletrack mountain biking.

Beaches

Raymer Bay & Bear Creek Regional Parks

Just down the road toward the lake, with public sandy beaches, swimming, picnic space, and boat launching.

Neighbourhood Parks

Westlake & Starlight Parks

Small pocket parks nestled into the neighbourhood streets, with sports courts and play structures.

Mountains & Outdoor Adventure

Behind the Neighbourhood

Rose Valley Mountain & Reservoir

Rocky bluffs rising directly behind the neighbourhood frame the skyline, with a reservoir hidden behind the ridges offering fishing spots and scenic viewpoints.

5 Minutes North

Bear Creek Provincial Park & Canyon

Canyon loop trails along rushing waterfalls, steep stair climbs, and campground access along Okanagan Lake.

Backcountry

McDougall Rim

Extends further up into the mountains behind the neighbourhood, with steep, long-distance hiking and some of the highest panoramic viewpoints over the central Okanagan.

Worth knowing: streets like Westlake Road, Horizon Drive, and Starlight Loop wind up bedrock hillsides, so driveways and side streets can be steep, and good winter tires and 4WD are standard here. Backing onto deep pine forest and Crown land also means deer, coyotes, and bears are a regular sight, and FireSmart landscaping is standard practice for homeowners up the hill.

A Sub-Area Within West Kelowna Estates

Rose Valley

Rose Valley is a highly sought-after, family-centric pocket tucked directly into the pine-covered hills immediately south of West Kelowna Estates, on the official MLS mapping. It mixes traditional single-family subdivisions from the late 1990s and 2000s with modern hillside craftsman builds and master-planned townhouse developments, attracting young families, active professionals, and outdoor enthusiasts.

Schools

Rose Valley Elementary and Mar Jok Elementary (both K–5), then Constable Neil Bruce Middle and Mount Boucherie Secondary.

Parks

Rose Valley Regional Park (250 hectares of hiking and singletrack around the protected Rose Valley Reservoir), plus local pocket parks like Rose Meadow and Rose Ridge.

Mountains

Rose Valley Mountain's rocky bluffs frame the backdrop, leading into Crown land trails up toward McDougall Rim.
