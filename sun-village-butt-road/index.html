Sun Village

A gated 45+ community in Westbank Centre, built around a landscaped waterway, with resort-style rancher and walkout homes.

About This Building

A gated 45+ community built around a waterway.

Sun Village, at 2330 Butt Road, is a gated 45+ community in Westbank Centre. Homes we've tracked are detached and semi-detached ranchers, several with full basements, set along a landscaped waterway that runs through the complex. Units range from roughly 1,583 sq ft on the main floor up to 3,320 sq ft total with a finished basement.

Sun Village is a leasehold community. Several listings we've tracked specifically note their individual unit's lease as "prepaid" or "fully paid" — but that's a per-unit detail, not a blanket statement about every home in the complex, so confirm the lease-payment status for any specific unit you're considering. We have not found explicit confirmation of whether the underlying land is Westbank First Nation land — don't assume either way. See our WFN leasehold guide for general leasehold-buying considerations in this area.
