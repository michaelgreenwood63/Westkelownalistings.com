Carbury

About This Complex

A brand-new townhouse community near Glen Canyon.

Carbury is a freehold townhouse community at 4025 Gellatly Road, built in 2025 — genuinely new construction, in the Glen Canyon / Gellatly Bay area of Westbank Centre. Real listings describe 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom townhomes with open-concept main floors, some end units reaching roughly 1,560 sq ft, and several listings reference developer GST-paid incentives on remaining inventory.

It's steps from the Powers Creek trail system and Glen Canyon Regional Park, with Okanagan Lake, parks, and beaches all a short distance away. Being freehold, it carries none of the land-lease considerations of nearby leasehold buildings like Aria or ABODE.
