Glenrosa

A mature, scenic, family-oriented neighbourhood on the southwest side of West Kelowna — the southernmost of the city's seven neighbourhoods.

About & History

A community with deep Okanagan roots.

Glenrosa is one of the neighbourhoods the City of West Kelowna points to as having roots dating back to the early-to-mid 19th century. That history sits alongside the modern city: West Kelowna itself only incorporated in 2007 (first as the Westside District Municipality, renamed West Kelowna in 2009, and recognized as a city in 2015) — so Glenrosa's residential streets predate the municipality that now governs them by generations.

Built on the gently sloping hillsides above Highway 97 and Powers Creek, Glenrosa's streets extend up Glenrosa Road into the mountain foothills. It mixes established 1970s-80s split-level family homes on large, mature lots with newer infill — modern farmhouse and craftsman-style single-family builds. Historically, it's offered some of the best value for space in West Kelowna, making it a staple for young families and first-time buyers wanting a backyard and a driveway.

Location & Geography

The southernmost neighbourhood, west of Highway 97.

West Kelowna's seven neighbourhoods really split east-west, not north-south: Lakeview Heights sits alone on the east side of Highway 97, on the benches above the lake, while the other six sit west of the highway. Glenrosa is the southernmost of those six — reached, along with neighbouring Smith Creek, via Westbank Centre's central corridor.

Powers Creek runs along Glenrosa's eastern border, cutting through a canyon of hiking trails and small waterfalls. Trepanier Provincial Park and Crown land border the neighbourhood to the west and north, offering direct access to wilderness, ridge walking, and pine forest.

It's a quick drive down to Highway 97 (under 5 minutes) and to Westbank Centre's grocery stores, banks, and dining, but the trip across the William R. Bennett Bridge into downtown Kelowna takes 20-25 minutes depending on traffic. Because Glenrosa gains real elevation climbing up Glenrosa Road, it sees more snow than the lower lakeside neighbourhoods — good winter tires matter here.
