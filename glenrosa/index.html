Glenrosa

One of West Kelowna's established residential communities, with roots reaching back to the area's earliest settlement.

About & History

A community with deep Okanagan roots.

Glenrosa is one of the neighbourhoods the City of West Kelowna points to as having roots dating back to the early-to-mid 19th century — among the oldest-settled pockets in the city, alongside Lakeview Heights. That history sits alongside the modern city: West Kelowna itself only incorporated in 2007 (first as the Westside District Municipality, renamed West Kelowna in 2009, and recognized as a city in 2015) — so Glenrosa's residential streets predate the municipality that now governs them by generations.

Today it's a settled, family-oriented part of the city — the kind of neighbourhood where buyers are choosing schools and streets as much as square footage.

Location & Geography

Southern West Kelowna, next to Mount Boucherie.

The City of West Kelowna groups its neighbourhoods into two clusters, and Glenrosa sits in the southern group — alongside Shannon Lake, Smith Creek, and Westbank Centre, which together hold roughly half the city's population. Glenrosa borders the Mount Boucherie area, giving it easy access to the hill's park and ball-field amenities.

Highway 97C, the Okanagan Connector, is the main route in and out of West Kelowna from the rest of the province — Glenrosa residents reach it via the city's core road network through Westbank Centre.
