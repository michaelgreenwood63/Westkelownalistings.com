The Residences at Lakeview Village

A newer condo development in West Kelowna's wine country, spanning two civic addresses on Olalla Road in Lakeview Heights.

About This Building

New construction in the heart of wine country.

The Residences at Lakeview Village is a newer condo development at 2760 and 2780 Olalla Road, in Lakeview Heights, the vineyard-benches neighbourhood overlooking Okanagan Lake. Listings describe it as new construction (“Brand New!” in several real MLS remarks), with multiple named buildings within the development — one confirmed as the “Mountain” building.

Units range from 1-bedroom + den layouts around 730 sq ft up to 2-bedroom + den homes over 1,250 sq ft, several with vineyard or lake-view decks. Exact total unit count and year of completion aren't independently confirmed yet; this will be updated once verified against the strata plan or City of West Kelowna permit records.
