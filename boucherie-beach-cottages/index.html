Boucherie Beach Cottages

A waterfront, cottage-style resort community at 2549 Boucherie Road — one of a small number of West Kelowna properties zoned to permit short-term rental.

About This Property

Waterfront, resort-zoned, built for short stays.

Boucherie Beach Cottages sits on the lake side of Boucherie Road in Westbank Centre, built around a cottage-style layout rather than a standard condo tower or townhome complex. Unlike most West Kelowna strata buildings, which require an owner to live on-site to rent short-term under the City's zoning bylaw, this property sits within West Kelowna's tourist commercial exemption — one of a short, named list of purpose-built resort and waterfront properties where short-term rental is permitted by zoning rather than treated as an exception.
