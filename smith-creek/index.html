Smith Creek

A quieter, smaller residential pocket in West Kelowna's southern neighbourhood cluster.

About & Context

One of West Kelowna's smaller, quieter corners.

Smith Creek is officially recognized by the City of West Kelowna as one of its named neighbourhoods, sitting alongside Glenrosa, Shannon Lake, and Westbank Centre in the city's southern grouping. It's a smaller, more low-key residential pocket compared to some of its higher-profile neighbours — we don't have a long published history or a list of dedicated landmarks to point to here, and we'd rather tell you that plainly than invent detail that isn't there.

What we can say with confidence: it benefits from everything the rest of southern West Kelowna offers — easy reach of Westbank Centre's shops and services, and the Mount Boucherie area's schools and recreation close by.

Location & Geography

Part of West Kelowna's southern cluster.

The City of West Kelowna's own neighbourhood map places Smith Creek in the southern grouping of communities — Glenrosa, Shannon Lake, Smith Creek, Westbank Centre, and Westbank — which together hold roughly half the city's population. It sits close to Glenrosa and Shannon Lake, with Westbank Centre's amenities and Highway 97C (the Okanagan Connector) both within easy reach via the city's core roads.

What's Nearby

Amenities in the Area

Smith Creek itself isn't home to a specific named school or park we can point to — but it sits within easy reach of amenities in the surrounding southern-cluster neighbourhoods.
