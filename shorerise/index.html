Shorerise

A lakeview custom-home community at 4055 Sand Glass Court — build-ready lots and pre-designed homes through Richmond Custom Homes.

About This Property

Lakeview lots, one builder, your design.

Shorerise is a lakeview custom-home community at 4055 Sand Glass Court in Westbank Centre, surrounded by other upscale custom homes. Lots here are tied to a single builder, Richmond Custom Homes — buyers can purchase a lot alone and build fully custom, or choose a pre-designed plan (the "Orion" walkout rancher from the Constellation Collection has been offered on listings here). GST applies on new construction.

This is a builder-tied lot community, not a strata corporation in the usual sense — talk to us about what that means for financing and timeline before you commit to a specific lot.
