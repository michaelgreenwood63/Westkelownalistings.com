Westbank Centre

The historic heart of the city — where "Westbank" began over a century ago, and still the civic and retail core of West Kelowna today.

History

The original Westbank.

A post office opened here in 1902 — the name "Westbank" was suggested by John Davidson, who had arrived in the district a decade earlier in 1892. For over a century, Westbank was the name for this entire side of the lake, right up until residents voted in 2008 to rename the newly incorporated municipality "West Kelowna" over "Westbank," by a narrow margin. Westbank Centre is where that original name — and the community itself — began.

The Westbank Museum keeps that history alive today, hosting guided tours of the adjacent Westbank Cemetery, which recently marked a century of remembrance, along with community events throughout the year.

Location & Geography

The civic and retail core of the southern cluster.

The City of West Kelowna groups Westbank Centre with Westbank, Glenrosa, Shannon Lake, Smith Creek, and Goats Peak/Gellatly in its southern neighbourhood cluster — together home to roughly half the city's population. Westbank Centre itself is the commercial anchor for that whole cluster.

Highway 97C (the Okanagan Connector) and the William R. Bennett Bridge make it the most convenient hub for both westbound travel and the short hop across the lake to downtown Kelowna.

Amenities

What's close by

Shopping & Dining

Retail outlets, cafes and restaurants, and tourist accommodations make this the day-to-day commercial hub for the whole area.

Parks & Recreation

Memorial Park (skateboard park and amphitheatre) and Westbank Town Centre Park sit right in the core. The Johnson Bentley Memorial Aquatic Centre — the city's main public pool — is also here.

Culture & History

The Westbank Museum and the century-old Westbank Cemetery, with guided tours and regular community events.
