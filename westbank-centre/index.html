West Kelowna · Southern Neighbourhoods

Westbank Centre

The historic heart of the city, where "Westbank" began over a century ago, and still the civic and retail core of West Kelowna today.

History

The original Westbank.

A post office opened here in 1902. The name "Westbank" was suggested by John Davidson, who had arrived in the district a decade earlier in 1892. For over a century, Westbank was the name for this entire side of the lake, right up until residents voted in 2008 to rename the newly incorporated municipality "West Kelowna" over "Westbank," by a narrow margin. Westbank Centre is where that original name, and the community itself, began.

The Westbank Museum keeps that history alive today, hosting guided tours of the adjacent Westbank Cemetery, which recently marked a century of remembrance, along with community events throughout the year.

Location & Geography

Flat, walkable, and right between the mountains and the lake.

Westbank Centre sits on flat terrain between the mountains and Okanagan Lake, west of Highway 97, functioning as the gateway you pass through to reach Glenrosa and Smith Creek, the two southernmost of West Kelowna's seven neighbourhoods. Shannon Lake, West Kelowna Estates, and Westside Road continue further north from there.

Unlike the hillside developments surrounding it, Westbank Centre is relatively flat and walkable, a dense blend of commercial plazas, civic facilities, established mid-century residential streets, and modern multi-family and condo developments. Supermarkets, bakeries, craft breweries, medical clinics, and financial institutions line Highway 97, Main Street, and Elliott Road, making it genuinely appealing to seniors, first-time buyers, and renters, not just commuters.

Highway 97 itself splits into a one-way couplet through downtown Westbank, Main Street westbound and Dobbin Road eastbound, which shapes traffic flow during peak hours. As the commercial centre of the city, it's also undergoing steady densification, with new multi-family housing replacing older lots.

Parks & Recreation
