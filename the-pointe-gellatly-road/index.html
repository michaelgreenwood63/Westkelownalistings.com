The Pointe

A freehold 55+ gated community at 4074 Gellatly Road, just above the boardwalk and shores of Okanagan Lake, with a full clubhouse and an active social calendar.

About This Community

Freehold, gated, genuinely amenity-rich.

The Pointe is a 55+ gated community at 4074 Gellatly Road — freehold ownership with no lease payments, confirmed directly in MLS® listing remarks. The community sits just above the boardwalk and shores of Okanagan Lake, with homes backing onto greenspace and canyon and mountain views in several units. Confirmed amenities include an indoor heated pool, a library, an exercise room, a billiards room, a hall with kitchen, and an outdoor BBQ area, with listings describing an active social calendar centred on the clubhouse.

Homes here are detached walk-out ranchers rather than attached condo units, which is less common for a 55+ strata community — several listings mention main-floor primary bedrooms, open-concept layouts, and finished walk-out basements.
