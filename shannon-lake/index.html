Shannon Lake

Built around the lake of the same name, and home to the only 18-hole golf course in the City of West Kelowna.

About & History

A lake, a golf course, and a settled residential grid.

Shannon Lake is named for the lake it surrounds, and it's best known locally for Shannon Lake Golf Course — the only 18-hole golf course within the City of West Kelowna. That single amenity shapes a lot of the neighbourhood's character, drawing golf-oriented buyers alongside families.

Like the rest of West Kelowna, it falls under a city that only formally incorporated in 2007 — but the neighbourhood itself has been a recognized residential community for considerably longer.

Location & Geography

Part of the southern cluster, inland from the lakeshore.

The City of West Kelowna places Shannon Lake in its southern neighbourhood grouping, alongside Glenrosa, Smith Creek, Westbank Centre, and Westbank — together home to roughly half the city's population. It's an inland community, built around its namesake lake rather than Okanagan Lake itself.

Highway 97C (the Okanagan Connector) remains the main route to and from the rest of the province, accessed through West Kelowna's core road network.
