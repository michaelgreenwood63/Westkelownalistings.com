Kara Vista

A freehold hillside strata community in West Kelowna Estates, designed around the natural landscape.

About This Building

Hillside living, designed to blend into the landscape.

Kara Vista, at 1870 Rosealee Lane, is a freehold strata community designed to maintain the natural hillside landscape it's built into. A unit we've tracked was a 3-bedroom, 4-bathroom walkout home with a two-level open-concept entrance, engineered hardwood, and a large granite kitchen island.

It's a freehold strata (not a land lease). Being on the hillside in West Kelowna Estates, units here commonly feature elevated views over the surrounding area.
