Knights View Estates

A gated collection of nine oversized estate lots at 1045 Bear Creek Lane, each a minimum of roughly 2.48 acres with unobstructed views of Okanagan Lake and Downtown Kelowna.

About This Property

Nine estate lots, built to a standard.

Knights View Estates is a gated, private bare-land strata community of nine oversized lots, each a minimum of roughly 2.48–2.49 acres, elevated off the water with unobstructed views of Okanagan Lake and Downtown Kelowna. Just a 10-minute drive to the city core, the lots back onto parkland to the west for lasting privacy. Access is via a paved private strata road, and lots are serviced with water, electrical, gas, and cable — prepared, flat building profiles ready for a sizeable estate-style home. Design guidelines apply to ensure a consistent standard of building quality while still allowing freedom of architectural style.

This is land for sale, not completed homes — buyers are purchasing a lot and building their own estate home to the community's design guidelines. Listings confirmed range from roughly $1.49M to $2.2M per lot.
