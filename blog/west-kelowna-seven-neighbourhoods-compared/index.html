Neighbourhood Guide

West Kelowna's Seven Neighbourhoods, Compared

West Kelowna isn't one uniform market. It's seven genuinely different pockets, and the real dividing line between them is Highway 97, running roughly east-west, not a north-south split. Here's what actually separates them.

East of Highway 97

Lakeview Heights is the only one of the seven on the east side, climbing the vineyard-covered benches directly above the lake. It commands a real premium for unobstructed water views, has roots dating to the early-to-mid 19th century, and sits just south of a Westbank First Nation reserve.

West of Highway 97, south to north

Glenrosa is the southernmost of the seven, a mature, family-oriented community on the hillsides above Powers Creek, with roots dating to the early-to-mid 19th century, its own elementary and middle schools, and panoramic views (not proximity) of Mount Boucherie and the lake.

Just north of Glenrosa, reached via the same Westbank Centre corridor, sits Smith Creek, the quietest of the seven, without a major standalone landmark, but with easy reach of everything its neighbours offer. Westbank Centre itself is the historic heart of the whole city, the literal site of the 1902 post office that gave this area its original name, "Westbank," still the civic and retail core today, with Memorial Park, the Johnson Bentley Memorial Aquatic Centre, and the Westbank Museum.

Continuing north, Shannon Lake is built around its own namesake lake and is home to the only 18-hole golf course in the City of West Kelowna. Further north still, West Kelowna Estates trades lake proximity for a quieter, elevated, inland setting. At the northern end, Westside Road is defined by its length, not a town centre. It runs along the lakeshore toward Fintry, with Bear Creek Provincial Park sitting directly alongside it. It's the furthest neighbourhood from Westbank Centre and Highway 97C.

The bottom line

If you want walkable civic life and history, look at Westbank Centre. If you want the view and don't mind paying for it, Lakeview Heights. If you want quiet and inland, Smith Creek or West Kelowna Estates. If you want the lake itself and some distance from everything else, Westside Road. Golf buyers know where to look already.
