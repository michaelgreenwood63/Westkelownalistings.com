West Kelowna's Seven Neighbourhoods, Compared

West Kelowna isn't one uniform market — it's seven genuinely different pockets, officially split by the city itself into a southern cluster (home to roughly half the population) and a northern one. Here's what actually separates them.

The southern cluster

Westbank Centre is the historic heart of the whole city — literally the site of the 1902 post office that gave this entire area its original name, "Westbank." It's still the civic and retail core today, with Memorial Park, the Johnson Bentley Memorial Aquatic Centre, and the Westbank Museum.

Glenrosa has roots dating to the early-to-mid 19th century and is anchored by its own elementary and middle schools, bordering the Mount Boucherie area for high school and recreation access.

Shannon Lake is built around its own namesake lake and is home to the only 18-hole golf course in the City of West Kelowna — a genuine draw for golf-oriented buyers.

Smith Creek is the quietest of the seven — a smaller residential pocket without a major standalone landmark, but with easy reach of everything its southern-cluster neighbours offer.

The northern cluster

Lakeview Heights climbs the vineyard-covered benches above the lake and commands a real premium for unobstructed water views — it's also one of the handful of neighbourhoods with 19th-century roots, and sits just south of a Westbank First Nation reserve.

West Kelowna Estates trades direct lake proximity for a quieter, more elevated, inland setting in the same northern cluster.

Westside Road is defined by its length, not a town centre — it runs north along the lakeshore toward Fintry, with Bear Creek Provincial Park sitting directly alongside it. It's the furthest neighbourhood from Westbank Centre and Highway 97C.

The bottom line

If you want walkable civic life and history, look at Westbank Centre. If you want the view and don't mind paying for it, Lakeview Heights. If you want quiet and inland, Smith Creek or West Kelowna Estates. If you want the lake itself and some distance from everything else, Westside Road. Golf buyers know where to look already.
